# Provisional title options

Professor approval is still required. The recommended working title is option 1.

1. **Long-Term Warming and Definition-Dependent Heatwaves in Dhaka, Bangladesh: Count Trends and Antecedent Meteorological Associations, 1972–2024** — Recommended because it names the location, period, principal methodological contribution, null-compatible trend framing, and qualified association analysis.
2. **Warming Without a Clear Persistent-Heatwave Count Trend in Dhaka, Bangladesh, 1972–2024** — Accurate and concise, but it foregrounds the null result more strongly than the full contribution.
3. **Heatwave-Definition Sensitivity and Persistent-Event Climatology in a 53-Year Dhaka Temperature Record** — Emphasizes the definition problem but understates the temperature-trend and association analyses.
4. **Daytime and Nighttime Warming, Persistent Heatwaves, and Antecedent Weather in Dhaka, 1972–2024** — Readable, but “antecedent weather” is less methodologically precise than “antecedent meteorological associations.”
5. **Contrasting Temperature and Heatwave-Count Trends in Dhaka: Evidence Across Absolute, Percentile, and Compound Definitions** — Highlights the central scientific contrast but omits the association analysis and exact study period.

Avoid “drivers,” “effects,” “human vulnerability,” “urban amplification,” “urban heat island,” and “projections” in the title because the current dataset does not identify causal mechanisms, health impacts, an urban–rural contrast, or validated long-horizon event forecasts.
